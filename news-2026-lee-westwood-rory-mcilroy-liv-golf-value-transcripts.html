  • Rory McIlroy questioned the value LIV Golf players had brought to their league on 19 August 2026 at the BMW Championship.

The exchange began with a press-conference question at the BMW Championship on 19 August 2026, where Rory McIlroy asked: "I would argue, have they brought value to LIV?" On 21 August, Sky Sports reported Lee Westwood's response from LIV Golf Indianapolis, calling McIlroy's remark "typical" and "not well thought out."

The complete transcripts reveal a nuanced debate over the definition of value. McIlroy evaluated LIV Golf's performance as a commercial league, whereas Westwood and Scottie Scheffler focused on the competitive quality and draw of individual golfers.
