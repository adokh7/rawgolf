The Most Welcoming Golf in America Happens at Charity Scrambles

For anyone intimidated by strict handicaps, exclusive dress codes, or country club etiquette, the charity scramble offers an open door. Structurally built around team camaraderie and community causes, these events represent the most welcoming format in golf.

BY GOLFRAW EDITORIAL PUBLISHED SUN 16 AUG 2026 GRASSROOTS GUIDE 4 MIN READ

Quick summary: While traditional tournament golf can feel intimidating to beginners and casual players, the charity scramble format is designed entirely around community, shared shots, and accessibility. By removing individual scoring pressure and opening private facilities to the public, charity outings remain golf’s most effective welcoming front door.

Why the Scramble Format Removes Intimidation

The scramble—or captain's choice—eliminates the pressure of poor shots. In this format, all four players tee off, the team selects the single best ball, and every member hits their next shot from that location. Topping a tee shot or missing a fairway carries zero team penalty if a teammate finds the short grass.

Combined with a simultaneous shotgun start, charity scrambles bypass the anxiety of crowded first tees and official starters, allowing newcomers to experience championship venues without individual scoring pressure.

"You don't need a single-digit handicap to help a team. Making one 10-foot putt or hitting one good drive is all it takes to contribute."
— GRASSROOTS GOLF DIRECTIVE

A Proven Model: The Ryan Taylor Memorial

The Ryan Taylor Memorial Golf Tournament in Williamsburg, Virginia—now in its seventh year across venues like Ford's Colony, Colonial Heritage, and Williamsburg National—embodies this structure. Established by Mary Brown in memory of her son Ryan, who passed away in 2019, the tournament donates 100% of proceeds to Faith Recovery, a residential addiction recovery facility.

With open entry conditions ($165 for singles, $650 for foursomes) providing access to premier private facilities alongside a post-round meal and reception, the tournament channels community participation into essential recovery resources.

How to Find Local Charity Golf Events

Because charity events are often organized locally rather than through central golf directories, finding them requires simple direct outreach:

  • Contact Pro Shops: Call local public and private courses to inquire about their outside outing and charity calendar.
  • Check Local Causes: Inquire with regional hospices, youth programs, and veteran organizations, which frequently host annual fundraisers.
  • Monitor Local News: Regional community papers and regional broadcasts regularly announce registration windows for upcoming scrambles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a charity golf scramble?

A team format where every player hits, the group selects the best shot, and all players hit their next stroke from that spot. It eliminates individual scoring pressure, making it ideal for beginners.

Do you need an official handicap to enter?

No. The vast majority of charity scrambles are open to all skill levels without requiring an established USGA or WHS handicap index.

What does registration typically include?

Entry fees generally cover 18 holes of golf with a cart, practice balls, a catered post-round meal or reception, and a direct donation to the sponsoring non-profit.

How does a shotgun start work?

Groups are assigned to different holes across the golf course and begin play at the exact same time, ensuring the entire field finishes concurrently for the awards reception.
