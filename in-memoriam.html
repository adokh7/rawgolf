In Memoriam

A record of the players and figures the game has lost, and of how we report a death in golf: what is confirmed, what is attributed, and nothing that isn't.

How GOLFRAW reports a death

Obituaries are the easiest place in golf writing to repeat something that turns out to be wrong. A death moves faster than its confirmation, and aggregation fills the gap with detail nobody sourced. Our rule here is the same one applied everywhere else on the site: a cause of death, a location, or a timeline appears only where a named source has stated it. Where reporting is thin, the piece says so rather than rounding the gap up into a narrative.
