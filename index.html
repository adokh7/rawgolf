What is GolfRaw?

GolfRaw is an independent golf news and PGA Tour analytics publication produced by GolfRaw Editorial. It combines verified tour data, course research, player-form analysis, and direct reporting to explain what happened, why it matters, and which popular narratives do not survive contact with the evidence.

What golf coverage does GolfRaw provide?

GolfRaw covers PGA Tour news, tournament previews, course architecture, renovations, player performance, strokes-gained trends, golf betting odds outlooks, swing analysis, historical features, and practical golf guides. Coverage focuses on active 2026 events while clearly separating current-season evidence from historical statistics and prior course configurations.

How does GolfRaw analyze PGA Tour courses?

GolfRaw analyzes PGA Tour courses by checking official scorecards, tournament media, club releases, and architect documentation before comparing player profiles. Yardage, par, routing, green shapes, hazards, and renovations are treated as model inputs, so old course history is never presented as directly comparable when the physical test has changed.

How does GolfRaw verify player statistics and form?

GolfRaw verifies player form with current PGA Tour statistics, ShotLink-fed data, official results, and clearly identified third-party models when used. The editorial desk checks dates, field strength, sample size, and season boundaries before drawing conclusions, and labels betting prices or projections as time-sensitive rather than permanent facts.

Who writes and publishes GolfRaw?

GolfRaw is written and published by GolfRaw Editorial, the site’s disclosed editorial organization. The desk uses an organizational byline instead of inventing individual experts, publishes its methodology and funding model, provides direct contact channels, and records confirmed factual errors in a permanent public corrections log.
