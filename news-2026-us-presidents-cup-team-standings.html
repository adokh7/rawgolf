2026 U.S. Presidents Cup team standings: Scheffler and Young secure automatic spots

2026 U.S. Presidents Cup team standings finalized 24 August. Collin Morikawa beat Chris Gotterup by 79 points. See who made the team and who needs a pick.

By GOLFRAW Editorial Published 24 Aug 2026 Updated 24 Aug 2026, 18:00 CEST 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • The six automatic qualifiers for the 2026 U.S. Presidents Cup team were confirmed following the BMW Championship on 24 August 2026.
  • Scottie Scheffler led the points standings wire-to-wire, joined by Xander Schauffele, Wyndham Clark, Patrick Cantlay, Sahith Theegala, and Collin Morikawa.
  • Collin Morikawa clinched the sixth and final automatic spot over Chris Gotterup by a razor-thin margin of 79 weighted points.
  • U.S. Captain Jim Furyk will select the remaining six captain's picks following the Tour Championship at East Lake.

The 2026 U.S. Presidents Cup automatic qualifying window officially closed on 24 August 2026 following the final round of the BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club. Six American golfers secured guaranteed roster spots for the upcoming clash against the International Team, locking in half of the 12-man squad captained by Jim Furyk.

World No. 1 Scottie Scheffler finished atop the rankings with an insurmountable points haul, while two-time major winner Collin Morikawa captured the final locked position by holding off a furious late push from Chris Gotterup in our tournaments hub.

Morikawa edges Gotterup in 79-point thriller

The battle for the sixth automatic qualification spot came down to the final holes at Bellerive. Entering Sunday on the bubble, Collin Morikawa posted a steady 2-under 68 to protect his position. Chris Gotterup, who contended throughout the weekend, fell just short by 79 weighted FedExCup points.

"Earning your way on points is always the goal. You never want to rely on a captain's pick when you have your fate in your own hands." Collin Morikawa after securing the No. 6 qualifying position

Gotterup's runner-up surge across August vaulted him to No. 7 in the final standings, putting him in prime position to be selected when Furyk finalizes his six captain's picks next week.

Captain's picks landscape for Jim Furyk

With six spots locked, U.S. Captain Jim Furyk will assess current form during the 2026 Tour Championship before announcing his remaining six wildcard selections. Chris Gotterup (No. 7), Russell Henley (No. 8), Brian Harman (No. 9), Cameron Young (No. 10), Max Homa, and Justin Thomas represent the core group under consideration.

Furyk noted during press availabilities that match-play temperament, course fit, and statistical strokes-gained metrics will carry equal weight alongside raw point standings.
