2026 U.S. Presidents Cup team standings: Scheffler and Young secure automatic spots

Scheffler, Young, Clark, Burns, Henley and Morikawa clinched automatic spots on Brandt Snedeker's 2026 U.S. Presidents Cup team. The picks and the result.

By GolfRaw Editorial Published 24 Aug 2026 UPDATED 15 NOV 2026 Updated 24 Aug 2026, 18:00 CEST 6 min read

Updated 15 November 2026. Key takeaways

  • Scottie Scheffler, Cameron Young, Wyndham Clark, Sam Burns, Russell Henley and Collin Morikawa earned the six automatic spots on the 2026 U.S. Presidents Cup team after the BMW Championship on 23 August.
  • Brandt Snedeker was the U.S. captain.
  • Snedeker’s six picks were Jacob Bridgeman, Patrick Cantlay, Chris Gotterup, Jackson Koivun, Xander Schauffele and Justin Thomas.
  • The U.S. won 17-13 at Medinah on 27 September, its 11th straight Presidents Cup.

The six automatic qualifiers

The top six in the U.S. standings after the BMW Championship qualified automatically, per the Presidents Cup: 1 Scottie Scheffler, 2 Cameron Young, 3 Wyndham Clark, 4 Sam Burns, 5 Russell Henley and 6 Collin Morikawa. Clark climbed into the top six by winning the BMW Championship at Bellerive.

Points were earned from 1 January 2025 through the 2026 BMW Championship, with 2026 events weighted more heavily, according to the official standings page.

Captain’s picks for Brandt Snedeker

Snedeker announced on 1 September that his six picks were Jacob Bridgeman, Patrick Cantlay, Chris Gotterup, Jackson Koivun, Xander Schauffele and Justin Thomas. Bridgeman, Gotterup and Koivun made their team debuts.

The result

The U.S. won the 2026 Presidents Cup 17-13 at Medinah on 27 September, its 11th straight victory over the International team, AP reported.
