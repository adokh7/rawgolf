Tiger Woods' Career Money List Record May Fall Today

Scheffler needs solo 13th, McIlroy needs solo 4th. One outlet already declared it done a week ago. Here's what's actually verified and what isn't.

BY GOLFRAW EDITORIAL · ON SITE, OAKMONT PA PUBLISHED FRI 13 JUN 2026 · 07:30 UPDATED 09:42 9 MIN READ

Key Takeaways

  • The Record: Tiger Woods holds the official PGA Tour career money record at $120,999,166, a mark he has held uninterrupted since 2000.
  • The Chasers: Scottie Scheffler trails by $609,000 (needs solo 13th today). Rory McIlroy trails by ~$3.2M (needs solo 4th today).
  • The Confusion: Discrepancies in media reporting prematurely crowned Scheffler last week. However, official tour metrics verify he is still short of Woods' historic number.
  • The Inflation: Sam Snead won 82 times and earned $620K in his career. Scheffler is on the verge of breaking the all-time record with just 21 victories.

The $609K Sunday Threshold

Heading into the final round of the Tour Championship, the math is staggering. Scottie Scheffler needs just $608,506 to eclipse Tiger Woods on the official career money list. Entering Sunday's revised tee times, Scheffler sits in a tie for third, well within the safety margin. If he finishes in solo 13th place or better (worth roughly $625,000), he claims the all-time record. Meanwhile, Rory McIlroy, starting tied for seventh, needs a massive surge to solo fourth to claim the record for himself.

The Media Discrepancy Breakdown

If you feel like you already read about this record falling, you aren't crazy. Following the BMW Championship last week, Yahoo Sports published a widely circulated piece claiming Scheffler had officially broken the record. This was premature. Yahoo had incorrectly tabulated unofficial money (likely FedExCup bonuses from prior years) into Scheffler's official career total. Golf Channel and the PGA Tour subsequently confirmed the verified official money math stands precisely at $120,390,661 for Scheffler, keeping Woods at the top.

McIlroy’s Shifting Reporting

Further clouding the record watch is Rory McIlroy's total. Different major outlets currently report discrepancies in McIlroy’s career earnings ranging from $117.2M to $118.1M. These variations stem from inconsistencies in how European Tour co-sanctioned events and early-career WGC payouts are retroactively calculated in official PGA Tour ledgers.

What the Official Money List Ignores

It is crucial to understand what "Official Career Earnings" actually means. It completely excludes bonus pools. If you factor in the $23 million FedExCup bonus pool and the $10 million Comcast Business Tour Top 10 regular-season bonus that Scheffler has already secured this year alone, his actual bank account has long surpassed Woods'. However, the official metric strictly tracks tournament purse payouts.

Historical Purse Inflation: Snead vs Woods vs Scheffler

The pace of modern purses renders historical comparisons absurd. Sam Snead amassed 82 PGA Tour victories and earned a career total of $620,126. Tiger Woods matched those 82 victories and pushed his earnings to $120.9 million—a staggering 195:1 ratio over Snead. Now, fueled by the Signature Event era, Scheffler is poised to surpass Woods' financial total in fewer than 168 career starts with only 21 victories. The era of the $20 million purse has entirely rewritten the record books.

Debunking 4 Money List Misconceptions

  • Myth 1: Scheffler already broke the record at the BMW Championship. False. Media reporting errors confused unofficial bonus money with official tournament payouts.
  • Myth 2: Tiger's earnings include his massive FedExCup bonuses. False. Official career money strictly excludes all season-long bonus pools for all players.
  • Myth 3: Tiger earned more internationally than on the PGA Tour. False. While Tiger commanded massive appearance fees globally, his $120.9M official PGA Tour total represents the vast majority of his competitive on-course earnings.
  • Myth 4: The Tour Championship payout is completely unofficial. False. Unlike the 2018 format, the 2026 Tour Championship awards official money ($10M to the winner) separate from the bonus structure.

The Raw Verdict

Scheffler will inevitably break the record, quite possibly this afternoon. While it highlights the astronomical rise in modern purses rather than a direct comparison of dominance to Woods, achieving $121 million in under 170 starts is a financial feat that defies comprehension.

FAQ

Who has the highest career earnings on the PGA Tour?

Tiger Woods currently holds the official PGA Tour career earnings record with $120,999,166, though Scottie Scheffler is less than $1 million behind.

Do FedExCup bonuses count toward career earnings?

No. The official PGA Tour career money list only tracks official tournament purse payouts, excluding season-long bonus pools like the FedExCup.
