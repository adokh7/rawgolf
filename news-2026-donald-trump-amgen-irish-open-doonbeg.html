Donald Trump attends Amgen Irish Open at Doonbeg

President Donald Trump is scheduled to attend the 2026 Amgen Irish Open at his Doonbeg golf course. The unprecedented visit brings massive security to County Clare.

By GOLFRAW Editorial Published 24 Aug 2026 Updated 24 Aug 2026, 17:20 CEST 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • Donald Trump will attend the 2026 Amgen Irish Open hosted at Trump International Golf Links Doonbeg in County Clare.
  • The DP World Tour selected the redesigned Atlantic links for its flagship national open following extensive Martin Hawtree renovations.
  • A massive multinational security operation involving the U.S. Secret Service, An Garda Síochána, and the Irish Air Corps is established across the Clare coast.
  • Local business leaders estimate an economic injection exceeding €20 million for the West Clare region.

Donald Trump will travel to Ireland to attend the 2026 Amgen Irish Open at his Trump International Golf Links resort in Doonbeg, County Clare. The tournament marks the first time the prestigious DP World Tour championship has been staged across the dunes of Doughmore Bay, placing one of the world's most high-profile golf properties under intense international sporting and political spotlights.

The visit brings unprecedented security protocols to the scenic coastal village, transforming the rugged links into a fortified championship venue while welcoming Europe's elite players to compete for a $6 million purse in our tournaments hub.

A landmark week for Trump International Golf Links Doonbeg

The selection of Doonbeg as host venue represents the culmination of a twelve-year investment and renovation plan undertaken by the Trump Organization. Since acquiring the property in 2014, the resort has invested heavily in coastal protection works, dune preservation, and a comprehensive course redesign overseen by acclaimed British architect Martin Hawtree.

"Doonbeg is one of the great natural wonders of links golf anywhere in the world. Having the Irish Open here is a huge honour for our team and for County Clare." Eric Trump, Executive Vice President of the Trump Organization

Hawtree's modifications resolved environmental erosion issues on the seaward holes and created several dramatic new greens, notably the par-3 14th hugging the Atlantic cliff edge. The setup will test DP World Tour professionals with unforgiving wind channels, deep sod-wall bunkers, and firm fescue fairways.

Unprecedented security measures across County Clare

The convergence of a head of state and a Tier-1 professional golf championship has triggered an extensive inter-agency security operation across Shannon Airport, Ennis, and the West Clare peninsula.

An Garda Síochána has deployed over 1,000 officers alongside specialized tactical units, dog squads, and maritime patrol vessels patrolling the waters of Doughmore Bay. The Irish Air Corps and the U.S. Secret Service have implemented a strict temporary restricted flight zone (TRA) extending 30 nautical miles around Doonbeg, prohibiting unapproved civilian aviation and drone operations.

  2. 22 August 2026: Irish Aviation Authority issued formal restricted airspace notices over West Clare.
  3. 24 August 2026: Security sweeps and perimeter magnetometers deployed across spectator entrance plazas at Doonbeg.

Player reactions and tournament stakes

The 2026 Amgen Irish Open serves as a crucial qualifying event for the upcoming Ryder Cup team points list and the season-ending DP World Tour Championship in Dubai. Leading European stars, including major champions and local favourites, have praised the turf conditions while preparing for the anticipated media storm.

Tour officials confirmed that all players will be insulated from political interactions, with dedicated press corridors and private transit routes ensuring the focus remains squarely on championship golf.

What readers are asking about Trump at Doonbeg

Is Donald Trump attending the 2026 Amgen Irish Open?

Yes. President Donald Trump has confirmed his schedule to attend tournament days at Trump International Golf Links in Doonbeg, County Clare during the 2026 Amgen Irish Open.

When did Donald Trump acquire the Doonbeg golf property?

The Trump Organization bought the Greg Norman-designed Doonbeg resort out of receivership in February 2014 for an estimated €15 million and subsequently redesigned several holes with architect Martin Hawtree.

What security arrangements are in place for the Irish Open at Doonbeg?

An Garda Síochána, the Irish Defence Forces, and the U.S. Secret Service have coordinated a ring of steel around the West Clare peninsula, featuring restricted airspace, marine coastal patrols, and secure spectator perimeters.

Has Doonbeg hosted the Irish Open before?

The 2026 championship marks the first time Trump International Golf Links Doonbeg has hosted the prestigious Amgen Irish Open on the DP World Tour.
