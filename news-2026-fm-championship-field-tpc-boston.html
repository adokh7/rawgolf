2026 FM Championship field: what the LPGA entry list actually shows

The LPGA closed entries for the 2026 FM Championship on 21 August. The entry list carries far more architectural detail than promotional taglines, revealing a $4.4 million purse and 34 reserve players waiting for a start.

Key Takeaways

  • The LPGA finalized the 2026 FM Championship field on 21 August 2026, ahead of competition at TPC Boston in Norton, Massachusetts (27–30 August).
  • The published entry list features roughly 129 entered players and 34 alternates on the reserve list.

Who Is in the 2026 FM Championship Field?

The LPGA's entries page lists Jeeno Thitikul at exempt rank 1 in the top-80 LPGA member category, followed by Miyu Yamashita at 2, Minjee Lee at 3, and Nelly Korda at 5. The tournament confirmed all ten golfers inside the top ten of the Rolex Women's World Golf Rankings have entered.

Crucially, exempt rank reflects priority access categories rather than form. Furthermore, with 34 reserve players on standby (roughly 26% of the field size), late withdrawals often reshape the bottom of the draw before Thursday's opening round.

Purse Growth & The 'Week Share' Metric

Working backwards from Miranda Wang's $615,000 winner's cheque in 2025 (assuming a standard 15% winner's share), the 2025 purse measured roughly $4.1 million. The 2026 boost to $4.4 million represents a $300,000 increase (+7.3%), putting the event firmly on track toward its $5 million commitment for 2028.
