Collin Morikawa Shot 61 at the Travelers and Still Lost by One

Collin Morikawa's nine-under 61 tied his career low and left him one shot out of a playoff. Viktor Hovland edged Scottie Scheffler on Monday morning. What actually happened, verified.

BY GOLFRAW EDITORIAL PUBLISHED SAT 15 AUG 2026 FINAL ROUND RECAP 4 MIN READ
Tournament Recap & Scoring Records: June 29, 2026 | Travelers Championship

Quick summary: Collin Morikawa closed the 2026 Travelers Championship with a breathtaking 9-under 61 on Sunday, posting nine birdies, zero bogeys, and tying his career low on the PGA Tour. Charging from eight shots back to set the clubhouse target at 20-under, it ultimately fell one stroke short of victory as Viktor Hovland defeated Scottie Scheffler in a Monday sudden-death playoff.

The Sunday Surge: Morikawa's Career-Low 61

Entering the final round at TPC River Highlands, Morikawa was an afterthought on the leaderboard, trailing eight shots behind the lead. What followed was one of the purest ball-striking and putting clinics of the 2026 PGA Tour season.

Morikawa dismantled the course with precision:

  • Back-to-back birdies on holes 1 and 2 to ignite the early charge.
  • An outward 30 (5-under) that erased five shots of deficit before the turn.
  • Three additional back-nine birdies at the 10th, 13th, and 16th to seize the clubhouse lead.
  • Zero dropped shots on a saturated layout battling afternoon storm systems.

Sandy Heroics in the Pouring Rain on 18

As Morikawa played the 72nd hole, the skies opened into a torrential downpour. After driving into the fairway bunker on the par-4 18th, most players would have played safely for par to lock in a top-five check.

"I was in between a wedge and a 9-iron with the ball below my feet. I went with the 9-iron and just flushed it through the rain."
— COLLIN MORIKAWA

His approach shot cut through the rain to settle inside nine feet. Under intense pressure and worsening conditions, Morikawa stepped up and drained the birdie putt to post 20-under 260, capping his 61 with the shot of the tournament.

The Putter Transformation: Gaining 6.5 Strokes on the Greens

While public perception often categorizes Morikawa as a master ball-striker burdened by an erratic flatstick, Sunday's performance completely inverted the narrative. The putter carried the entire final-round assault:

  • 1st in the field in Strokes Gained: Putting on Sunday.
  • +6.5 strokes gained on the greens alone over 18 holes.
  • ~135 feet of putts made, including crucial conversions from 30, 31, 17, and 15 feet.
  • 25 total putts, converting nearly every makeable chance inside 20 feet.

Weather Suspension, the Monday Playoff, and the Title

Just minutes after Morikawa signed his scorecard, dangerous electrical storms forced PGA Tour officials to suspend play at 5:57 p.m. ET. The 83-minute delay pushed regulation play into the gloaming, where Scottie Scheffler and Viktor Hovland matched 21-under totals to force extra holes.

With darkness setting in, tournament officials scheduled the sudden-death playoff for Monday morning at 9:00 a.m. ET on the 18th hole. Hovland converted on the first playoff hole to capture the title and the $3.6 million winner's share, leaving Morikawa in solo third, exactly one agonizing stroke out of the playoff.

The Big Picture: Form Heading Into the Summer

While falling one stroke short is bitter, Morikawa's final round was anything but wasted. Competing in his third consecutive tournament week while managing a lingering back issue, posting the lowest score of the championship demonstrated that his complete game is peaking for the FedExCup Playoffs and the major championships.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who won the 2026 Travelers Championship?

Viktor Hovland won the tournament, defeating Scottie Scheffler on the first hole of a sudden-death playoff on Monday morning after both tied regulation at 21-under par.

What did Collin Morikawa shoot in the final round?

Morikawa shot a nine-under 61 (nine birdies, zero bogeys), tying his career-low round on the PGA Tour and recording the lowest score of the final round.

Did Morikawa qualify for the playoff?

No. Morikawa finished in solo third place at 20-under par, missing the Hovland-Scheffler playoff by a single shot.

How did Morikawa birdie the 72nd hole?

He struck a 9-iron approach from a wet fairway bunker in heavy rain to nine feet and rolled in the birdie putt to post 20-under.

Why was the playoff moved to Monday?

A severe 83-minute weather suspension on Sunday evening pushed the completion of regulation play near dusk, forcing officials to hold the sudden-death playoff Monday at 9:00 a.m. ET.

The Raw Take

Morikawa's 61 is proof of what happens when generational ball-striking meets a white-hot putter. Gaining over six strokes on the greens in a single round from eight shots behind showed true championship grit. He forced the world No. 1 and the eventual champion to play flawless golf to beat him by one. If this putting form holds, Morikawa will be the most dangerous player in the FedExCup Playoffs.
