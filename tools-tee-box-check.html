02 · Why This Matters

What tees should I play?

Start from the Tee It Forward chart that the PGA of America and the USGA published in 2011. It matches average driving distance, carry plus roll, to an 18-hole length: a 200-yard drive suggests 5,200 to 5,400 yards, a 250-yard drive 6,200 to 6,400. Plenty of golfers are further back than that. A 2023 USGA study found about half of male golfers and three quarters of female golfers play tees too long for them.

The approach shot is the whole argument

Tee boxes exist so that different players face the same second shot. A par 4 is designed to be a drive and a mid-iron. If you are standing over 190 yards on every par 4 while the course architect intended a 9-iron, you are not playing the hole as designed — you are playing a much harder hole that nobody built.

Moving up is not cheating

It is the single cheapest improvement available to you. No lesson, no new driver, no range sessions. Just teeing off 500 yards further forward and suddenly hitting wedges into greens. Your handicap does not care which coloured post you started from.

How many shots is it really worth?

The USGA’s Course Rating formulas give a fair guide. On the men’s formulas, every extra 200 yards adds about 0.9 strokes to a scratch golfer’s rating and about 1.25 to a bogey golfer’s; the women’s formulas give about 1.1 and 1.7. Play 600 yards too far back as a bogey golfer and that is close to four strokes a round, more than most golfers gain from a winter of lessons.

03 · Method & Sources

How the estimate works

Your driver carry becomes an estimated total drive, which is read against the Tee It Forward chart in a straight line between its rows and rounded to 50 yards. The chart gives a 200-yard band for each drive, so the answer is a range, not a single number.

  • Course length: PGA of America and USGA, Tee It Forward guidelines, 2011. Its driving distance is carry plus roll, as the R&A and USGA’s 2019 playing-lengths study reads it.
  • Carry to total: USGA, D. Pierce, recreational golfer distance study, 1 December 2023 (627 golfers): driver carry = −34.38 + 1.03 × total.
  • Stroke cost: USGA Course Rating length formulas: a scratch rating rises 1 stroke per 220 yards, a bogey rating 1 per 160 (men). Your typical score places you between the two: 72 or better counts as scratch, 92 or worse as bogey. That placing is our simplification.
  • Approach and club: an average par 4 is taken as about 6% of the course (our approximation); the club comes from The Distance Check’s estimate for your driver.
  • Who plays too far back: USGA Green Section Record, D. Pierce, June 2023 (the Best Tees research).

Yards or metres: the chart is published in yards, so the calculator converts a carry in metres to yards, reads the chart, and converts the answer back. Both units give the same course; only the rounding differs.

A second opinion

The USGA’s newer 7-iron tee selector, from its 2023 Best Tees research, starts from your 7-iron instead and can suggest a longer course than this chart. If the two disagree, try both sets for a few rounds and keep the one where you reach more greens.

Limitations

  • The chart only knows length. Forced carries, elevation, altitude, wind, firm or soft ground and hole design all change how long a course plays.
  • Roll depends on your flight and the turf, so the total drive is an estimate.
  • The stroke cost uses the men’s formulas for everyone; the women’s formulas charge about a quarter to a third more per yard. The formulas stop at bogey golfers, so for higher scores the real cost is probably larger.
  • Yards only.
FAQ · Questions Golfers Actually Ask

What tees should I play?

Start from your average driving distance, carry plus roll. The PGA of America and USGA Tee It Forward chart suggests about 5,200 to 5,400 yards for a 200-yard drive and 6,200 to 6,400 for a 250-yard drive. Treat it as a starting point, then adjust for how many greens you reach.

How many shots does playing the wrong tees cost you?

The USGA Course Rating formulas suggest about 0.9 strokes for every extra 200 yards for a scratch golfer and about 1.25 for a bogey golfer (men’s formulas; the women’s are higher). Playing 600 yards too far back costs a bogey golfer close to four strokes a round.

Is moving up a tee box cheating?

No. Tee boxes are designed so that players of different distances face the same approach shots. Playing tees that are too long means hitting long irons into greens built for wedges.

What is the Tee It Forward chart?

A 2011 guide from the PGA of America and the USGA that matches average driving distance to an 18-hole course length, from 2,100 to 2,300 yards for a 100-yard drive up to 6,700 to 6,900 for a 275-yard drive. This calculator reads it for your drive, between rows in a straight line.
