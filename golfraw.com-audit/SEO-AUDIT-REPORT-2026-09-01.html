GOLFRAW.COM SEO AUDIT

1 September 2026

GOLFRAW is crawlable and largely indexable, with 338 live sitemap URLs, HTTPS preferred URLs on sitemap pages, current news feeds, and useful publisher trust signals. The main risk is a template and URL consistency problem: 11 live indexable routes contain stale Oakmont article output, while two sets of internal links point to 404 pages. This audit covers technical SEO, indexing, performance signals, page content, links, structured data, news discovery, Discover readiness, architecture, and content priorities.

Where the site stands

The basic crawl foundation is sound. robots.txt allows crawling and declares the sitemap, every one of the 338 sitemap URLs returned a direct 200 response without a redirect, every sitemap page has one title, one meta description, one canonical (preferred URL), and one H1 (main page heading), and the sitemap contains no noindex URLs.

The highest-impact problem is page integrity. On 11 current live indexable routes, the visible H1 and body still show the Oakmont article while the title, URL, image, or structured data describe another story. Repairing that data-to-template path and the related broken URLs should come before broad content expansion.

If you only do one thing

Repair the article data-to-template pipeline and purge stale Oakmont output from the 11 live indexable routes.

Google and readers can receive a page whose title, visible heading, lead image, preferred URL, and machine-readable article data disagree. That weakens relevance, trust, sharing, and news distribution on the affected pages.

  1. Audit the 11 routes first: the 10 sitemap URLs /news-2026-hovland-leads-tour-championship-final-day, /news-2026-hovland-one-shot-lead-tour-championship, /news-2026-michael-block-lead-ally-challenge, /news-2026-michael-block-leads-ally-challenge-final-round, /news-2026-pga-tour-winners-2026, /news-2026-tiger-woods-career-money-list-record, /news-2026-tour-championship-final-round-hovland-leads, /news-2026-tour-championship-round-3-tee-times-leaderboard, /news-2026-tour-championship-sunday-tee-times-round-4, and /why-pros-are-ditching-hybrids, plus the live legacy route /news-every-shot-tiger-woods-80th-win-2018.
  2. Fix the content lookup or template data binding so each route has matching visible H1, standfirst, byline, date, lead image, title, canonical, Open Graph URL, and Article JSON-LD (machine-readable page data). Remove the Oakmont fallback or stale fixture data.
  3. Fetch each corrected URL as a logged-out user, then check the rendered HTML and Google Search Console URL Inspection after Search Console is connected. Confirm that no affected page contains the Oakmont H1 and that each preferred URL returns 200 with matching article data.

Small fixes, in order

Stale article output high

Eleven current live indexable routes show the visible H1 Oakmont Is Eating the Field Alive even when their title, canonical, lead image, and JSON-LD (machine-readable page data) describe another story. For example, /news-2026-pga-tour-winners-2026 has a winners title and canonical but an Oakmont H1 and standfirst.

Fix: Correct the article data source or template fallback, regenerate the affected HTML, and validate the five-way match between H1, title, lead image, canonical, and JSON-LD. Recheck social previews and the visible byline after deployment.

Broken preferred URLs and links high

/news-every-shot-tiger-woods-80th-win-2018 returns 200 but its canonical, Open Graph (social sharing metadata) URL, JSON-LD page ID, and breadcrumb point to /every-shot-tiger-woods-80th-win-2018, which returns 404. Three links on the Tiger career-money article use that 404 path. Three links on /why-pros-are-ditching-hybrids use /tools/bag-audit, which returns 404 while /tools-bag-audit returns 200.

Fix: Keep /news-every-shot-tiger-woods-80th-win-2018 as the preferred URL unless a replacement page is deliberately created. Point its canonical, Open Graph URL, JSON-LD, breadcrumb, and internal links there, and include it in the sitemap if it should rank. Replace all /tools/bag-audit links with /tools-bag-audit, or add a permanent redirect.

Image delivery and Core Web Vitals risk high

Current measurements do not include field LCP (largest content paint), INP (interaction responsiveness), or CLS (layout shift) because Search Console is not connected and PageSpeed Insights returned a quota response. The live sitemap pass found 527 image instances without explicit width and height across 153 pages and 191 without responsive srcset variants. The homepage has 21 images, 20 without dimensions, 19 without srcset, and 5 without lazy loading. The local public asset directory is about 82 MB and contains 48 image files above 300 KB.

Fix: Add width and height or an equivalent aspect-ratio box to every image, especially homepage cards. Generate responsive 400, 800, and 1200 pixel variants with srcset and sizes. Keep the first visible editorial image eager or preloaded, lazy-load below-fold images, compress the largest assets, then measure mobile LCP, INP, and CLS in Lighthouse and Search Console. Google’s targets are LCP within 2.5 seconds, INP below 200 ms, and CLS below 0.1.

Article schema consistency medium

The 338-page live pass found 649 parseable JSON-LD blocks and no malformed blocks. Of 304 article pages, 15 lack an Article JSON-LD author, 5 lack a JSON-LD image, and 5 lack dateModified. BreadcrumbList data is absent on 18 core hub and trust pages, including /news, /pga-tour, /liv-golf, /guides, /analysis, and /ratings.

Fix: Standardize one article schema component with headline, concise description, crawlable image, author with author URL, publisher logo, datePublished, dateModified when materially updated, and mainEntityOfPage. Add visible breadcrumbs and matching BreadcrumbList data to indexable hubs and trust pages, excluding the homepage where appropriate.

News discovery and Discover readiness medium

/feed.xml is current, with a 1 September 2026 build date, 15 items, publication dates, links, descriptions, and image enclosures. A recent article has NewsArticle data, visible byline and date, a 1200 by 675 image, max-image-preview:large, and source links. The normal sitemap has 338 URLs but 51 lack lastmod, and there is no dedicated news sitemap. Discover inclusion and performance cannot be measured because Search Console is not connected. Readiness signals are present.

Fix: Populate accurate lastmod values for all changed pages, based on the real editorial update time. Consider adding /news-sitemap.xml for articles published in the last two days if dedicated News reporting and freshness tracking are useful, with publication name, publication date, and title fields. Submit it in Search Console and keep the normal sitemap for the full site. Continue using relevant representative images at least 1200 pixels wide, above 300,000 pixels total, preferably 16:9, with max-image-preview:large.

Thin and placeholder pages medium

The indexable /full-board page has 143 words in the crawl and mainly presents a ratings table. The noindex pages /past-issues and /the-card have 54 and 63 words and placeholder or signup-focused content. The /analysis hub has 253 words and /ratings has 374 words. The sitemap contains 338 unique URLs, but this audit did not establish a complete duplicate-body comparison outside the confirmed stale Oakmont template group.

Fix: Expand /full-board with methodology, data source, update cadence, rating interpretation, limitations, and FAQs, or keep it as a utility page and set it to noindex. If it becomes noindex, remove it from the sitemap while keeping it available for users and internal links. Keep the newsletter and empty archive pages noindex until they contain a useful archive or editorial explanation. Expand the Analysis and Ratings hubs with clear topic summaries and links to their strongest pages.

Titles and search snippets medium

All 338 sitemap pages have a title and meta description. Using 60 characters for titles and 160 for descriptions as screening thresholds, 64 titles and 26 descriptions exceed them. Examples include the 66-character title on /news-2026-end-of-season-driver-deals, the 174-character description on /news-2026-bryson-dechambeau-pga-tour-return, and the 173-character description on /guides-most-valuable-golf-search-cpc-data.

Fix: Create title and description rules for each page type. Put the primary topic and useful differentiator first, then the brand. Rewrite the 64 longest titles and 26 longest descriptions by hand or with a reviewed template, preserving article facts and avoiding truncation that removes the subject.

Navigation and topic architecture medium

The homepage primary navigation links to Latest News, PGA Tour, LIV Golf, Tournaments, Guides, Search, and Tools. Analysis and Ratings are available lower on the page or in the footer, while the current sitemap contains 244 news-style routes among 338 URLs. The crawl shows strong internal linking on hubs, including 295 internal links on /news, 143 on /pga-tour, and 57 on /guides.

Fix: Add Analysis and Ratings to a persistent primary or “Data” navigation group. Make each hub link to its best evergreen guides, tools, methodologies, and current stories, and add related-story or related-tool links on articles. Keep useful existing article URLs; consolidate only genuinely overlapping stories after reviewing traffic and backlinks.

Redirect and header polish low

The apex domain (golfraw.com without www) HTTP to HTTPS to www path currently takes two redirects, while the direct www HTTPS URL returns 200. HSTS (browser HTTPS policy) is present. The reviewed response headers did not include Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy, or Permissions-Policy.

Fix: Collapse the apex redirect chain to one permanent redirect into https://www.golfraw.com/. Add security headers after checking compatibility with Google Tag Manager, HubSpot, ads, and other third-party scripts. Treat this as infrastructure polish after the page integrity and image work.

Where to focus first

Build the next evergreen content cluster around golf swing analysis and evaluation. It matches GOLFRAW’s existing Analysis hub and tools, has clearer low-difficulty opportunities than broad golf news terms, and can support both search traffic and useful internal journeys.

golf swing analysis 2,900 searches/mo

Saved US keyword research from 29 August estimates KD (keyword difficulty) 15. The existing /analysis hub is a natural destination but is only 253 words in the crawl.

Make: Turn /analysis into the hub: define the workflow, explain what the tools measure, link to swing articles and tools, and show when each method is useful.

best golf swing analysis app 880 searches/mo

Saved US keyword research estimates KD7. GOLFRAW already has /golf-swing-analysis-apps, so this is the fastest page-level opportunity.

Make: Refresh the page with a comparison table covering use case, inputs, output, free versus paid access, privacy, and limitations. Add a visible reviewed date, methodology, and links to the relevant GOLFRAW tools.

golf swing evaluation 320 searches/mo

Saved US keyword research estimates KD7. The query reflects a practical problem and can be served by an evergreen guide rather than a news post.

Make: Create a dedicated guide explaining what to record, a repeatable self-evaluation checklist, common swing patterns, and when to use a coach or video tool. Link it to the Analysis hub and Tendency Engine.

Already working

  • Crawl access is clear. robots.txt allows crawling and names https://www.golfraw.com/sitemap.xml.
  • Sitemap hygiene is strong. The 338 URLs are unique, direct 200 responses, and none of the sitemap pages carries noindex.
  • Basic page metadata is present. Every sitemap page has one title, description, canonical, and H1 in the live pass.
  • The current homepage HTML document is materially improved. It is about 96.7 KB and has no video tag, so the former large homepage video finding is stale.
  • Publisher and article signals are useful. The homepage includes NewsMediaOrganization policies and recent articles include bylines, dates, source links, article schema, and large preview images.
  • Alt text coverage is complete in the live sitemap pass. The remaining image work is dimensions, responsive variants, file weight, and correct page-to-image matching.
