DOCUMENT P-04 THE CORRECTIONS LOG POLICY NOTHING DELETED, EVER

Every mistake. In public. Forever.

Every publication makes mistakes. Most bury them. This page is the opposite: every error we've published, what we got wrong, when we fixed it, and who caught it — newest first, preserved permanently. We can't promise we'll never be wrong. We can promise you'll never have to wonder.

How this ledger works Errors are logged within 24 hours of confirmation. The original mistake stays visible, struck through — memory-holing is the coward's edit (Tenet T3). Severity is graded: FACTUAL (wrong stat, wrong quote, wrong claim) or MINOR (typos, broken links, mislabeled dates). Whoever catches it gets named, unless they'd rather not be. Catch one yourself: contact@golfraw.com. Confirmed catches are logged here, with your name if you want it.
