DOCUMENT P-04 THE CORRECTIONS LOG POLICY NOTHING DELETED, EVER

Every mistake. In public. Forever.

Every publication makes mistakes. Most bury them. This page is the opposite: every error we've published, what we got wrong, when we fixed it, and who caught it — newest first, preserved permanently. We can't promise we'll never be wrong. We can promise you'll never have to wonder.

How this ledger works Errors are logged within 24 hours of confirmation. The original mistake stays visible, struck through — memory-holing is the coward's edit (Tenet T3). Severity is graded: FACTUAL (wrong stat, wrong quote, wrong claim) or MINOR (typos, broken links, mislabeled dates). Whoever catches it gets named, unless they'd rather not be. Catch one yourself: contact@golfraw.com. Confirmed catches are logged here, with your name if you want it.
ERR-044 LOGGED 15 NOV 2026 FACTUAL
LIV golfer disqualified at DP World Tour Q School
We wrote “Four over on Thursday. One better on Friday’s second round. Then nothing.” Also: under Rule 5.3a, “past five minutes, disqualification is mandatory”; and the no-show “makes it look like a decision rather than an accident”.
The truth Peter Uihlein shot 74 and 73 and missed his Friday third-round tee time (Field Level Media). Rule 5.3a has an exception when the Committee finds exceptional circumstances. No source supports the suggestion that the no-show was deliberate.
Caught by GolfRaw Editorial (self-reported).
ERR-043 LOGGED 15 NOV 2026 FACTUAL
Jack Nicklaus career earnings vs the modern PGA Tour
We wrote Nicklaus, Palmer and Player “won 104 majors’ worth of golf between them for less than four modern paydays”; Nicklaus said they “never really became wealthy from playing golf”.
The truth They won 34 majors, and their combined official PGA Tour earnings ($9.43 million) equal almost six of Jackson Koivun’s $1.584 million cheques. We could not find the “never really became wealthy” remark in any published source.
Caught by GolfRaw Editorial (self-reported).
ERR-042 LOGGED 15 NOV 2026 FACTUAL
Blades Brown at 19
We wrote “After an opening 72, Brown blitzed The Dunes Golf and Beach Club with a 10-under 61 in the second round, matching the lowest score by a 16- or 17-year-old in PGA Tour history”, gaining “over 3.5 strokes on approach”. Also: U.S. Amateur rounds of 64-72 at Cherry Hills, a Tennessee State Open title, and a top-125 card threshold.
The truth No such round happened. His 2024 Myrtle Beach rounds were 72-67-66-69 (PGA Tour), and his lowest PGA Tour round is a 60 at the 2026 American Express. He shot 72 at Cherry Hills and 64 at Colorado Golf Club, was low amateur (not champion) at the 2023 Tennessee Open, and full status goes to the top 100 from 2026.
Caught by GolfRaw Editorial (self-reported).
