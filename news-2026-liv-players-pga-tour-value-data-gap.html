Do LIV players add value to the PGA Tour? The data gap

Rory McIlroy questioned what defecting golfers had brought to LIV Golf. Television figures support his criticism of the league, while Brooks Koepka's return exposes a different question: what can an individual star actually add to the PGA Tour?

BY GOLFRAW EDITORIAL PUBLISHED THU 20 AUG 2026 MEDIA AUDIT 5 MIN READ
Sports Media & Economics: August 20, 2026 | PGA Tour vs. LIV Golf Ratings & Player Valuation

Key Takeaways

  • McIlroy questioned what LIV golfers had brought to LIV Golf rather than stating that every individual LIV player lacks drawing power.
  • The PGA Tour averaged 3.1 million Sunday viewers against LIV Golf's 175,000 across seven overlapping 2025 broadcasts (a 17.78x gap).
  • Brooks Koepka accepted stringent financial and competitive conditions to return, including lost equity eligibility and a $5 million charitable contribution.
  • Evaluating future potential returnees like Jon Rahm or Bryson DeChambeau requires isolating individual marginal value from broad league averages.

Do LIV players add value to the PGA Tour? The public record cannot settle that question with a single broad stroke. Rory McIlroy's remark before the BMW Championship fits LIV Golf's struggle to build broadcast scale, while Nielsen's reported 17.78-fold television gap measures two tours and leaves the marginal commercial pull of Jon Rahm or Bryson DeChambeau unmeasured.

"Conflating a league's weak broadcast distribution with an individual golfer's marquee drawing power is an economic mistake."
— MEDIA RIGHTS AUDIT

What Did Rory McIlroy Say About LIV Player Value?

Speaking at Bellerive ahead of the BMW Championship on 19 August 2026, Rory McIlroy was asked whether returning LIV golfers would bring value to the PGA Tour. His response was precise: "I mean, I would argue, have they brought value to LIV? You know?" He subsequently described the PGA Tour as operating from a position of strength.

That phrasing directly challenges the commercial return on investment delivered to LIV Golf. It does not prove that marquee names lack individual drawing power. By contrast, Scottie Scheffler argued that top LIV performers could add tangible value to tournaments, even while endorsing penalties for defectors seeking reinstatement.

The 17.78x Audience Gap vs. Individual Star Power

Across seven head-to-head Sundays in 2025, PGA Tour coverage averaged 3.1 million viewers on CBS and NBC compared to 175,000 for LIV Golf across Fox, FS1, and FS2—a 17.78-to-1 ratio according to Nielsen Big Data + Panel measurements.

While LIV Golf's massive spending (reportedly exceeding $5 billion) failed to build linear television ratings, individual returnees still drive notable interest. Koepka's return at the Farmers Insurance Open helped drive Golf Channel's average audience to 542,000 viewers, with ESPN featuring his opening rounds on its main channel and Disney+ platforms.

How the PGA Tour Evaluates Returnees (The 5-Point Test)

  1. Competitive Contribution: Does the golfer elevate field strength, OWGR point allocations, and Signature Event depth?
  2. Audience Demand: Does the player generate measurable, repeatable spikes in linear TV ratings, streaming hours, and digital engagement?
  3. Attributable Revenue: Can commercial teams tie title sponsorship renewals and ticket sales to their presence?
  4. Strategic Fit: Does the acquisition strengthen international media rights and fan acquisition?
  5. Institutional Cost: What penalties, equity forfeitures, and field expansions are required to maintain trust among loyal PGA Tour members?

Frequently Asked Questions

Did Rory McIlroy say LIV golfers have no value?

No. McIlroy questioned whether the golfers had brought value to LIV Golf. That is a criticism of the league's business growth, not a literal claim that individual players possess zero commercial draw.

Can other LIV golfers still return to the PGA Tour?

The PGA Tour's Returning Member Program closed on 2 February 2026. Any future returns would require a brand-new policy framework or qualification pathway.

Did Brooks Koepka's return boost PGA Tour ratings?

Koepka's return coincided with a 75%+ year-over-year viewership surge for the Farmers Insurance Open, although broadcast distribution and scheduling also factored into the gains.

Are Jon Rahm or Bryson DeChambeau returning to the PGA Tour?

No agreement exists for either player. Both remain under LIV Golf contracts, with Rahm having recently captured his third straight LIV individual season title.

The Raw Take

LIV Golf's broadcast ratings prove that star rosters alone cannot manufacture a dominant media property from scratch. However, conflating a league's weak distribution with an individual golfer's worth is a mistake. On the PGA Tour's established platform, marquee stars like Rahm, DeChambeau, and Koepka still command substantial attention—making the terms of any future return an economic equation rather than an emotional argument.
