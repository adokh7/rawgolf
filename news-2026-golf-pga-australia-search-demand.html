What Do Australians Actually Search for in Golf?

They mostly search for the American tour. In a 1,140-row Australian keyword export for the seed term golf PGA, 9 rows contain the word Australia or Aussie. Widen the filter to include state and capital city names and the count rises to 15 rows (1.3%). The rest is leaderboards, tee times, television listings, and who won on the US circuit.

Total stated volume across all 1,140 rows is 5,659,060 monthly searches. The 9 Australia-naming rows total 6,060 (0.107%). The single biggest entry is golf pga tour at 165,000 searches a month. Two of the largest remaining entries, golf pga leaderboard and golf pga tour leaderboard, sit at 18,100 each—queries typed by someone in an Australian time zone trying to track US action.

When and Where Is the 2026 BMW Australian PGA Championship?

The 2026 BMW Australian PGA Championship will be played at The Lakes Golf Club in Sydney from 26 to 29 November 2026. The New South Wales Government confirmed the venue and dates in a ministerial media release dated 17 April 2026. The PGA of Australia owns and runs the championship, co-sanctioned by the DP World Tour.

Why Social Platform Metrics Are Flawed in Keyword Exports

Of the 73 keywords in the 1,140-row export that appear across multiple platforms, 60 carry an identical volume figure on every platform (82.2%). The query golf pga tour is reported at 165,000 a month on Google, Bing, YouTube, and TikTok identically.

This "platform mirror" indicates tools applying search engine volume across social tags. Furthermore, all 33 distinct non-zero volume values match Google Ads Keyword Planner's standard rounding buckets.

How Long Has It Been Since Sydney Hosted the Australian PGA?

Twenty-eight years. The New South Wales Government release of 17 April 2026 and ABC News on 3 August 2026 both date the last NSW staging to 1998 at New South Wales Golf Club (won by David Howell). The Lakes Golf Club itself last hosted in 1987 (39 years ago)—two separate historical figures that coverage often conflates.

PGA TOUR vs. PGA Championship vs. Australian PGA

Three separate organisations share the acronym:

  • PGA of Australia: Operates the Challenger PGA Tour of Australasia and the Australian PGA Championship.
  • PGA of America: Runs the PGA Championship (men's major) in the United States.
  • PGA TOUR: Runs the week-to-week US circuit and FedExCup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the 2026 Australian PGA Championship being played?

The 2026 BMW Australian PGA Championship will be played at The Lakes Golf Club in Sydney from 26 to 29 November 2026, co-sanctioned by the DP World Tour.

When did the Australian PGA Championship last visit New South Wales?

In 1998 at New South Wales Golf Club (a 28-year gap). The Lakes Golf Club specifically last hosted the championship in 1987 (39 years ago).

Is the Australian PGA Championship the same as the PGA Championship?

No. The PGA of Australia runs the Australian PGA Championship, while the PGA of America runs the US-based men's major PGA Championship.
