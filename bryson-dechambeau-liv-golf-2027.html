Bryson DeChambeau After LIV Golf: What’s Confirmed About 2027

LIV Golf is winding down, and the DP World Tour has offered him full status for 2027. As of 15 November he had not announced a decision.

By GolfRaw Editorial Published 03 Sep 2026 Updated 15 Nov 2026 5 min read

Updated 15 November 2026. We first published this on 3 September, when reports said DeChambeau was “all in” on a relaunched LIV 2.0. That league is not happening: LIV is winding down. We have rewritten the page around what is confirmed now and removed our earlier analysis of LIV 2.0 purses and format, which no longer applies. Several source links in the original pointed to pages that do not exist or to publishers’ home pages; every claim below now links to the reporting it comes from. See the Corrections Log.

Key takeaways

  • LIV 2.0 is off. LIV’s lawyer told the bankruptcy court on 5 November that it is moving to a wind-down.
  • Europe is open to him. The DP World Tour has offered full status for 2027 if he applies, with a mid-November deadline.
  • No decision is public yet. As of 15 November he had not announced where he will play.
  • The PGA Tour is not an option until August 2027. LIV players are barred for a year from their last LIV start.

Where things stand

LIV Golf filed for Chapter 11 on 8 September. On 5 November, its lawyer told the bankruptcy court that “the debtors are not currently proceeding with the LIV 2.0 plan” and were “toggling to a value-maximizing wind-down” (AP). A month earlier, LIV had announced financing from BC Partners Credit, part of a targeted $300 million, to emerge as a team league with player equity (AP). That plan is the one that has now been dropped.

For DeChambeau, that removes the league he was widely expected to stay with. More of the LIV story is on our LIV Golf desk and in our Chapter 11 explainer.

The DP World Tour offer

The Telegraph reported, as carried by Golf Channel on 5 November, that his team was told he would have “full playing privileges on the European circuit in 2027” if he applies. The offer falls under Category 1, which covers major and Race to Dubai winners from the past seven years; he won the 2024 U.S. Open.

The deadline is reported two ways: 14 November by Golf Channel, and 15 November by Sky Sports. Sky also reports that because he did not take up membership in 2025 and 2026, he has lost his exemption for 2028 and 2029. As of 15 November, we found no report of a decision.

What he has said

After LIV’s wind-down was announced, DeChambeau said in a statement: “I’m not going to rewrite the last four years. I made a decision I believed in, and I’m grateful for what came with it.” On what comes next, he said he would “go about whatever is next the right way” (AP; GOLF.com). He did not name a tour.

The PGA Tour route

The PGA Tour bars LIV players for one year from their last LIV appearance (AP). Sky Sports reports that these players will not be eligible to play on the PGA Tour until August 2027.

He had an earlier way back and passed on it. In January the Tour opened a Returning Member Program for four players, Brooks Koepka, Jon Rahm, DeChambeau and Cameron Smith, with a 2 February deadline. The Tour called it “a one-time, defined window” (Golf Channel). Koepka returned; DeChambeau said, “I’m contracted through 2026, so excited about this year” (theScore). Our earlier piece on his PGA Tour comments covers that period.

How we got here

  • 12 January: the PGA Tour announces the Returning Member Program; the window closes on 2 February (Golf Channel).
  • Late August: his LIV contract ends after the season finale in Indianapolis, and he posts “It was a good run” on Instagram (theScore; Gulf News).
  • Early September: reports relayed from Spanish outlet Ten Golf say he is “all in” on LIV 2.0. He never confirmed it, and on 29 September Alan Shipnuck reported he was “going back and forth” (Field Level Media).
  • 8 September: LIV files for Chapter 11 (AP).
  • 5 October: LIV announces BC Partners Credit financing for a relaunch (AP).
  • 5 November: LIV tells the court it is winding down instead (AP).
  • 8 November: DeChambeau’s statement, with no destination named (AP).
  • 14–15 November: the reported DP World Tour deadline (Golf Channel; Sky Sports).

Frequently Asked Questions

Has Bryson DeChambeau decided where he will play in 2027?

Not publicly, as of 15 November 2026. The DP World Tour has offered him full status for 2027 if he applies, with a deadline reported as 14 November (Golf Channel, citing the Telegraph) or 15 November (Sky Sports). On 8 November he said he would "go about whatever is next the right way" without naming a tour (AP).

Is LIV Golf continuing in 2027?

No. LIV filed for Chapter 11 on 8 September 2026, and on 5 November its lawyer told the bankruptcy court it was "not currently proceeding with the LIV 2.0 plan" and was moving to a wind-down (AP).

Can DeChambeau return to the PGA Tour?

Not before August 2027. The PGA Tour bars LIV players for one year from their last LIV appearance (AP), and Sky Sports reports these players are not eligible until August 2027. He passed on the Tour's one-time Returning Member Program, which closed on 2 February 2026 (Golf Channel).

Why does he have a DP World Tour option?

He is a major champion. The Telegraph, via Golf Channel, reports his offer falls under Category 1, which covers major and Race to Dubai winners from the past seven years. Sky Sports reports he lost his exemption for 2028 and 2029 by not taking membership in 2025 and 2026.
