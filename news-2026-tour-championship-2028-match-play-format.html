The Tour Championship 2028 Match Play Format, Explained Without the Spin

The PGA Tour just rebuilt its finale around match play. Here is how the 2028 format actually works, and the flaw Scottie Scheffler pointed at.

By GOLFRAW Editorial Published 27 Aug 2026 Updated 27 Aug 2026, 17:20 CEST 8 min read

Key takeaways

  • Starting in 2028, the PGA Tour finale transitions to a two-week match play format, featuring a 32-man group stage followed by a 16-man single-elimination bracket.
  • The FedExCup regular season and playoff points bonus will be awarded before match play begins, insulating full-season earnings from single-match volatility.
  • Scottie Scheffler voiced the fundamental critique of match play: high single-match variance can eliminate a player shooting 64 against a 63, while a player shooting 74 advances.
  • Rory McIlroy and Tiger Woods strongly endorsed the pivot, citing head-to-head Sunday television drama, clear winner narratives, and fan bracket engagement.
  • East Lake's permanent hosting monopoly ends; the 2028 championship series will rotate annually across top-tier championship venues.

The PGA Tour has finally embraced match play for its ultimate prize. Under the 2028 two-series competitive overhaul spearheaded by PGA Tour leadership, the Tour Championship will abandon 72-hole stroke play entirely. In its place comes a dramatic, two-week head-to-head postseason designed to deliver the one thing professional golf broadcasts desperately crave: unambiguous, high-stakes match play with survival on the line every afternoon.

The transition is the centerpiece of the PGA Tour's 2028 restructuring. But behind the glossy television promos and bracket pool excitement sits a fundamental philosophical tension that Scottie Scheffler laid bare in the East Lake media center.

How the 2028 Match Play Finale Actually Works

The 2028 finale is structured as a progressive two-week Championship Series, whittling down the tour's top performers through distinct competitive phases.

The top 90 players in the primary PGA Tour series qualify for the postseason. Following an opening 90-man stroke-play cutoff, the field is trimmed to the top 32 qualifiers who advance to the match play championship:

The Flaw Scottie Scheffler Pointed At

Scottie Scheffler did not mince words when asked about crowning the season champion through 18-hole matches. His critique went straight to the mathematical heart of match play variance.

“Match play is unbelievable entertainment for fans, and I love playing it in the Ryder Cup,” Scheffler remarked. “But if you're asking whether match play identifies the best golfer over an entire season, the math simply doesn't support that. You can shoot 64 on Friday and lose 2&1 because your opponent shot 63. Meanwhile, in the group next to you, a guy shoots 74 and wins 1-up because his guy shot 75. One of you goes home and one advances.”

"You can shoot 64 and go home while someone shoots 74 and advances. Match play is great drama, but it is not a pure test of who played the best golf all year." Scottie Scheffler · East Lake Media Availability

Scheffler's observation is supported by decades of statistical modeling. In standard 72-hole stroke play, a world-class player's cumulative talent edge asserts itself over 288 shots. In an 18-hole match, a hot putter or two fortuitous bounces can easily eliminate the world No. 1 in 90 minutes of golf.

Why McIlroy and Woods Pushed for the Change

Despite Scheffler's concerns, player directors and broadcast executives viewed the move as essential for the modern commercial era. Rory McIlroy and Tiger Woods were instrumental in shaping the 2028 format.

“Golf is the only major sport where the championship finish line frequently turns into a procession,” McIlroy explained during tour briefings. “In stroke play, if someone holds a six-shot lead on Sunday, the final two hours lose competitive tension. Match play guarantees that Sunday is a cage match. Every shot matters, every hole has immediate consequences, and you get iconic head-to-head duels that casual sports fans actually tune in to watch.”

Broadcast & Commercial Drivers

  • Bracket Engagement: March Madness-style bracket tournaments drive unprecedented digital interaction and sports betting liquidity.
  • Guaranteed Sunday Climax: An 18-hole championship match guarantees that the winner is decided on the final green in direct competition.
  • Star Matchups: Group play ensures that every marquee player is guaranteed at least three full days of television coverage on Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday.

The Separation That Makes It Work

The crucial mechanism that made the 2028 format palatable to tour players was the total separation of the season points bonus from the match play tournament purse.

Under the new rules, the FedExCup points champion—the player who displayed unrivaled excellence from January through August—is crowned and paid their massive bonus check before the match play bracket begins. The 2028 Tour Championship then stands on its own as an elite, high-stakes match play tournament with its own trophy, official winner's check, and career exemptions.

The End of East Lake's Monopoly

The 2028 overhaul also marks the end of East Lake Golf Club as the permanent, unchanging venue for the finale. While Atlanta's historic course remains an integral part of the rotation, the championship will travel across major venues including Pebble Beach, Pinehurst, Riviera, and TPC Sawgrass, providing varied architectural tests suited for match play theatre.

The Golf Raw Take

Scottie Scheffler is right about the math, and Rory McIlroy is right about the show.

Golf does not need another four-day stroke play event with quiet leaderboards and staggered start times. By paying the season-long champion their $20+ million check upfront and throwing 32 of the world's most aggressive golfers into an unyielding match play cage match for the finale, the PGA Tour finally created something television viewers will actually stop scrolling to watch.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 2028 Tour Championship match play format work?

The 2028 finale features a two-week postseason structure: Week 1 consists of 32 players in eight 4-man round-robin groups, and Week 2 is a single-elimination 16-player bracket culminating in an 18-hole championship match.

What was Scottie Scheffler's criticism of the 2028 match play format?

Scheffler highlighted that match play contains high individual variance where a player shooting 64 can be eliminated by a 63 while another player shooting 74 advances against a 75, making it an imperfect determinant of full-season excellence.

How is the FedExCup points champion handled in 2028?

The FedExCup points champion is crowned and awarded the season-long bonus pool prior to the match play finale, separating regular-season dominance from the standalone match play tournament title.

Will the Tour Championship leave East Lake Golf Club in 2028?

Yes. Starting in 2028, the PGA Tour will rotate the championship series across premier championship venues rather than keeping East Lake as the sole permanent host.
