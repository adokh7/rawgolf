# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Committed amateur golfers using GolfRaw's tools on mobile and desktop, often during or immediately after a round. They need quick, legible inputs and trustworthy analysis without disrupting play.

## Product Purpose

GolfRaw combines blunt golf journalism with browser-based tools that help golfers understand their game, compare performance, assess course fit, and keep a private local record. Success means a golfer can complete a tool quickly, understand the result immediately, and return to it without relearning the interface.

## Positioning

GolfRaw pairs an unfiltered editorial voice with practical golf analytics that run entirely in the browser and keep the golfer's data on their own device.

## Operating Context

The interactive tools are used one-handed on phones as well as on desktop. Some workflows happen on the course, while score and tendency analysis often happens just after a round. The Locker persists bags, profiles, rounds, and exports locally across tools.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, custom CSS, and vanilla JavaScript deployed on Vercel.
- Existing calculations, data structures, local storage behavior, SEO content, URLs, structured data, and editorial copy must remain intact.
- Interactive controls must remain keyboard accessible and meet a 44px minimum touch target where practical.
- The Tendency Engine, Field Reader, and shared Locker are priority surfaces, while all interactive tool pages must share the redesigned system.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve GolfRaw's blunt, editorial tone and existing brand name.
- Premium modern sports analytics quality comparable to The Athletic, Arccos Golf, and Whoop.
- Deep tournament forest green is the primary accent; warm neutral canvas and crisp status colors support it.
- Avoid the incumbent brutalist heavy-border treatment.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the live SEO copy, tool logic, tournament field data, local Locker implementation, self-hosted Archivo and IBM Plex Mono fonts, and current production assets. No new performance, customer, or commercial claims may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action obvious at a glance.
- Keep dense golf data scannable without dumbing it down.
- Treat mobile tap quality as a first-class product feature.
- Preserve local-first privacy and analytical honesty.
- Let GolfRaw's voice carry the personality while the interface stays disciplined.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, sufficient color contrast, and touch targets suitable for one-handed use.
