The 'No Cut' Paradox: 40% of the Field Goes Home

Run the numbers and the conventional phrasing collapses: 50 players start, 30 advance, and 20 are eliminated. A standard 36-hole cut trims the field for the weekend but eliminates nobody from the overarching season. Bellerive allows everyone to play 72 holes, yet terminates the season for 40% of the field on Sunday afternoon.

Simply qualifying for this 50-man field secures full exemptions into all eight 2027 PGA Tour Signature Events—guaranteeing immense career security even for those who fail to reach Atlanta.

The Bubble Battleground

Entering Round 1, Rickie Fowler, Bud Cauley, and Kurt Kitayama occupied the high-stress bubble positions (28th through 30th), hotly pursued by Gary Woodland, J.T. Poston, and Alex Noren. With 750 points awarded to the winner and substantial points distributed across the top 10, a single strong round completely recalibrates the standings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a cut at the 2026 BMW Championship?

There is no 36-hole cut. All 50 players complete four rounds at Bellerive. However, only the top 30 in FedExCup points advance to the Tour Championship at East Lake.

How many players advance to the Tour Championship?

Thirty players qualify for the season finale at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta, where all 30 start at even par.

What time is the first tee at the 2026 BMW Championship?

The first group (Justin Thomas and Sahith Theegala) teed off at 10:03 a.m. ET (9:03 a.m. CDT) on Thursday 20 August 2026.

How much does last place earn at the BMW Championship?

Last place (50th) earns at least $66,000 from the $20 million purse, while the winner takes home $3.6 million and 750 FedExCup points.
