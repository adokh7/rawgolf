3M Open Prize Money Payout 2026: How Much Will the Winner Earn at TPC Twin Cities?

BY GOLFRAW EDITORIAL PUBLISHED SUN 26 JUL 2026

The Quick Verdict

The short answer is this: if the 2026 3M Open purse is $8.8 million, the winner at TPC Twin Cities will take home about $1.584 million. That number comes from the PGA Tour’s usual payout structure for regular full-field events, where first place typically receives 18% of the total purse. So while the official payout sheet still needs to confirm the final breakdown, the expected winner’s check is easy to estimate. For players still battling late in the season, that is a serious payday — and one of the biggest reasons the 3M Open continues to matter far beyond just the trophy.

How much will the 2026 3M Open winner make?

If the total purse is indeed $8.8 million, the projected first-place prize is:
$1,584,000
That is the number most fans are looking for, and it would make the 2026 champion one of the biggest earners of the week on the PGA Tour.

Why the winner’s payout is projected at $1.584 million

This projection comes from the standard PGA Tour formula used in most regular events.
Take 18% of an $8.8 million purse, and you get:
$1,584,000
Unless the Tour releases a different event-specific payout table, that is the most reasonable estimate for what the winner will earn at TPC Twin Cities.

How it compares with last year

If last year’s purse was $8.4 million, the winner’s share would have been $1.512 million.
That means a jump to an $8.8 million purse would raise the first-place prize by:
$72,000
It is not a massive leap in the grand scheme of PGA Tour money, but it is still a meaningful increase for the player who gets the job done on Sunday.
