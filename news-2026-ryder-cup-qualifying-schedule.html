Ryder Cup 2027: Team Europe Qualifying Schedule Confirmed — British Masters to Kick Off Race to Adare Manor

The road to Adare Manor begins August 27. Here's everything you need to know about Team Europe's 2027 qualifying system, key dates, points structure, and who to watch.

BY GOLFRAW EDITORIAL PUBLISHED TUE 04 AUG 2026

The road to the 2027 Ryder Cup at Adare Manor officially begins later this month. Ryder Cup Europe confirmed on August 4, 2026 that the qualification process for Team Europe will start at the Husqvarna British Masters hosted by Sir Nick Faldo, taking place at The Belfry from August 27–30.

It's a fitting venue to launch the campaign. The Belfry has hosted the Ryder Cup four times — in 1985, 1989, 1993 and 2002 — and Sir Nick Faldo, who hosts the event, is one of the most capped players in the competition's history.

How the 2027 European Qualifying System Works

The 2027 process retains the single Ryder Cup Points List format introduced for the 2025 cycle, replacing the old dual European Points List and World Points List system.

Key details:

  • Qualifying window: August 27, 2026 – August 22, 2027
  • Automatic qualifiers: Top 6 from the Ryder Cup Points List
  • Captain's picks: 6, announced by Luke Donald in the week commencing August 30, 2027
  • Team size: 12 players

Eligible DP World Tour members will earn points across both DP World Tour and PGA TOUR events throughout the year-long campaign. The specific tournament banding structure — which determines how many points are available at each event — will be confirmed when both tours release their 2027 schedules later this year.

The 2025 System as a Guide

While the exact 2027 points structure hasn't been published yet, the 2025 qualification campaign offers a useful reference:

Tournament Category Points Available (2025)
Major Championships 5,000
PGA TOUR Signature Events / The Players / FedExCup Playoffs 3,000
DP World Tour Rolex Series 2,000
PGA TOUR regular FedExCup events 2,000
DP World Tour 'Back 9' events 1,500
DP World Tour 'Global Series' events 1,000
PGA TOUR 'Opposite' events 1,000

The 2025 system also restricted PGA TOUR points eligibility to events not scheduled against DP World Tour Rolex Series events, with one exception (the ISCO Championship, played opposite the Genesis Scottish Open). Points were awarded at The Zurich Classic with 50% allocated to each player, but only if both were European.

Luke Donald: Chasing History as Three-Time Captain

Luke Donald will captain Team Europe for the third consecutive Ryder Cup — the first player to do so since Bernard Gallacher led the side in 1991, 1993 and 1995.

Donald's record speaks for itself: victories at Marco Simone in 2023 and Bethpage Black in 2025. A win at Adare Manor would make him the first European captain since Tony Jacklin (1985, 1987, 1989) to win three consecutive matches.

"The start of qualification is always a notable milestone in the Ryder Cup journey," Donald said in the official Ryder Cup Europe announcement. "We have a lot of talented European players at the moment, some with Ryder Cup experience and others who will be trying to qualify for the first time. I am therefore really looking forward to seeing them compete to qualify for Team Europe over the next 12 months."

Adare Manor: A Centenary Celebration

The 2027 Ryder Cup carries extra significance. It marks the 100th anniversary of the competition, first played in 1927 at Worcester Country Club in Massachusetts, where Walter Hagen's United States defeated Ted Ray's Great Britain 9½–2½.

Adare Manor in County Limerick becomes only the second Irish venue to host the Ryder Cup, following The K Club in 2006 — where Europe, captained by Ian Woosnam, recorded a record-equalling 18½–9½ victory.

"Next year's Ryder Cup at Adare Manor promises to be very special for so many different reasons," Donald added. "It will be the 100-year anniversary of the Ryder Cup and we will have the support of the Irish crowds, who are among the best in the world. Those factors will only add to the motivation of our European players to ensure they are part of it."

Key Dates for the 2027 Ryder Cup

  • August 27–30, 2026: Qualifying begins at the Husqvarna British Masters, The Belfry
  • August 22, 2027: Qualification period ends
  • Week commencing August 30, 2027: Luke Donald announces six captain's picks
  • September 13–16, 2027: Practice days at Adare Manor
  • September 17–19, 2027: Competition days — Friday to Sunday

Who to Watch in the Qualifying Race

With a year of golf still to play, it's too early to identify favourites. But the 2025 team — featuring Rory McIlroy, Robert MacIntyre, Tommy Fleetwood, Justin Rose, Tyrrell Hatton and Rasmus Højgaard — provides a strong core.

McIlroy, a five-time major champion, has been vocal about his desire to win a Ryder Cup on home soil. Hatton and Højgaard's eligibility will depend on their continued DP World Tour membership, given their involvement with LIV Golf — a factor that remains subject to ongoing negotiations between the tours.

Emerging talents like Nicolai Højgaard, Ludvig Åberg and Matt Wallace will also be targeting a first or second appearance, while veterans like Shane Lowry — who narrowly missed automatic qualification in 2025 — will be determined to be part of the Irish homecoming.

What's Still Unknown

  • Exact points structure: The tournament banding for 2027 hasn't been published. Will it mirror 2025, or will there be adjustments?
  • Final qualifying events: The specific DP World Tour and PGA TOUR events that conclude the qualification period on August 22, 2027 will be confirmed when 2027 schedules are released.
  • LIV Golf eligibility: Whether players like Jon Rahm and Tyrrell Hatton can maintain DP World Tour membership — and therefore Ryder Cup eligibility — remains unresolved.
  • PGA TOUR points window: Whether the 2027 system will allow PGA TOUR points from the start of the campaign, or restrict them to the new calendar year as in 2025.

The Bigger Picture: Europe's Bid for Three in a Row

Europe hasn't won three consecutive Ryder Cups since 2010–2014 (when they won three straight under Colin Montgomerie, José María Olazábal and Paul McGinley). A victory at Adare Manor would cement this era as one of the most successful in European Ryder Cup history.

The qualification process starting at The Belfry — a venue steeped in European Ryder Cup folklore — feels symbolic. Faldo, who played in 11 Ryder Cups and captained the 2008 side, remains one of the competition's most iconic figures.

For the players, the message is clear: the race to Adare Manor starts now.
