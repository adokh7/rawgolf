LIV Has Cancelled Its Biggest Event of the Year. It's Still Selling Tickets For It.

LIV Golf has informed its players that the $40 million Michigan Team Championship, scheduled for August 27 to 30 at The Cardinal at Saint John's Resort in Plymouth, will not take place. Tickets remain available through LIV's official website, with no public cancellation announcement.

BY GOLFRAW EDITORIAL PUBLISHED WED 12 AUG 2026 · 21:30 BREAKING 5 MIN READ

LIV GOLF BREAKING NEWS: August 12, 2026. GolfRaw understands Michigan is off. The private notice closes weeks of doubt for the people due to play, yet paying fans have been left looking at a very different version of events.

At the time of publication, LIV's official event page still listed the August 27 to 30 dates, displayed a countdown and linked to a ticket portal. Sales were open. Grounds passes and hospitality products sat beside the buy button, even though LIV had made no public statement explaining the cancellation or what ticket holders should do next.

Fox Sports had also removed Michigan from its upcoming broadcast plans, according to information supplied to GolfRaw, leaving Indianapolis as the final scheduled stop of the season. The buy button remained.

How Michigan Reached This Point

Signs had accumulated for nearly a month before the player notice landed, and each one made the public-facing ticket page look stranger.

  • Mid-July: Cleeks GC captain Martin Kaymer called the Team Championship "highly unlikely" after executive briefings failed to settle his concerns about LIV's plans.
  • Late July: Another warning arrived. Reporting found no event infrastructure at Saint John's Resort.
  • August 9: Broadcast commentary during the Bedminster tournament identified Indianapolis as the season's last stop, even though Michigan remained on the public calendar.
  • August 12: Players received the formal cancellation notice.

Money sharpens the problem. Michigan carried a $40 million team prize fund, roughly £30 million, with $11.2 million previously earmarked for the winning squad and a full season of team points feeding into the event.

Who Wins the Team Title Now?

Nobody has given the players a clear answer, with 4Aces GC leading on 149.50 points, Ripper GC close behind on 147.25 and Legion XIII on 145. Those margins gave Michigan a proper competitive purpose. Removing it leaves three teams separated by less than five points and no published mechanism for settling the title before Indianapolis.

The purse is unresolved too. LIV has not told players whether the $40 million will be paid through the season standings, folded into Indianapolis or withheld. A league built around team golf has reached its final competitive week without saying how its team champion will be chosen.

Players know Michigan is off. Ticket buyers can still pay to attend, with no answer on the team title or its $40 million purse.

Indianapolis Becomes the Finish Line

Indianapolis ends the season. The Club at Chatham Hills in Westfield, Indiana, hosts the final scheduled event from August 20 to 23. LIV's original 14-event plan has shrunk to 12 played events after cancellations in Louisiana and Michigan, and Indianapolis was designed as the last regular event before the team finale. Now it carries both jobs.

The empty Michigan week overlaps with the British Masters at The Belfry, so LIV players already eligible for the DP World Tour event would no longer have a league date in the way. Entries and releases will decide that.

The cancellation comes as LIV promotes a signed lead investor for 2027 and follows an unusual Bedminster week in which the winning team finished six over par. For now, the league has kept the public ticket page alive after telling its players Michigan is gone.

The Raw Take

Canceling an event creates refunds, changed travel plans and angry customers. Leaving the buy button live compounds all three. LIV can fix the page quickly, but ticket holders still need straight answers about refunds, the team champion and the money promised for Michigan. They are still waiting for an official email.
