What Time Does Scottie Scheffler Tee Off?

Scottie Scheffler begins Round 1 at 2:33 p.m. ET (1:33 p.m. CDT local) paired with Matt Fitzpatrick. His pairing is the 23rd of 25 groups off the first tee, followed only by Russell Henley/Jacob Bridgeman and Maverick McNealy/Matt McCarty.

Note on pairings: Both Fitzpatrick brothers are in the field. Matt Fitzpatrick plays with Scheffler at 2:33 p.m. ET, while Alex Fitzpatrick plays with Kristoffer Reitan earlier at 11:27 a.m. ET.

What Time Does Rory McIlroy Tee Off?

Rory McIlroy tees off at 1:20 p.m. ET (12:20 p.m. CDT local) alongside major champion Xander Schauffele as the 17th pairing off the 1st tee, giving fans an uninterrupted look at both marquee groups.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time does Scottie Scheffler tee off in Round 1?

Scottie Scheffler tees off at 2:33 p.m. ET (1:33 p.m. CDT) alongside Matt Fitzpatrick on Thursday 20 August 2026.

What time does Rory McIlroy tee off in Round 1?

Rory McIlroy tees off at 1:20 p.m. ET (12:20 p.m. CDT) playing with Xander Schauffele, 73 minutes ahead of Scheffler.

Are Scheffler and McIlroy paired together for Round 1?

No. The PGA Tour split the duo following their pairing at TPC Southwind, placing McIlroy with Schauffele and Scheffler with Fitzpatrick.

Did Scottie Scheffler play in the 2018 PGA Championship at Bellerive?

No. Scheffler had just turned professional in 2018 and did not qualify for the 2018 PGA Championship at Bellerive.
