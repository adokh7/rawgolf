The Most Valuable Golf Search in America Gets 40 Hits a Month

An analysis of 944 unique search terms in the US golf market reveals a striking inversion: high-volume commodity queries like "black friday golf deals" carry low advertiser value ($0.95 CPC), while obscure niche queries like "golf deals fairmont st andrews" command $9.06 per click on just 40 monthly searches.

BY GOLFRAW EDITORIAL PUBLISHED WED 12 AUG 2026 · 12:00 5 MIN READ

Forty searches. That is the estimated monthly US demand behind "golf deals fairmont st andrews," yet advertisers pay an estimated $9.06 for each click. "Black friday golf deals" draws 3,600 searches and costs about $0.95 per click.

Data Breakdown: Intent Clusters vs. Advertiser Value

The June 2026 dataset combines exports from Google, Bing, YouTube, Amazon and social platforms, and after duplicate and irrelevant terms were cleaned out, 944 unique golf searches remained. Grouping those searches by intent exposes the same pattern found in our earlier analysis of what Americans mean by "golf deals": domestic bargain hunting brings scale, while international trip planning brings expensive clicks.

Why Overseas Travel Queries Command Premium Pricing

Florida and Myrtle Beach lead. A search for "golf deals Florida" or "Myrtle Beach packages" may come from a local golfer comparing tee times, a family browsing resorts or a traveler with no fixed dates. That broad demand produces a $1.58 median CPC across the US destination cluster.

Fairmont St Andrews is a different purchase. A golfer who names the resort is already narrowing a trip that may include flights, lodging and several rounds, and the Fairmont query reaches $9.06 per click while "golf deals Portugal" reaches $8.62 and Scotland queries reach $7.68.

Exclusions and Data Noise

Raw keyword exports pull in several businesses and products that contain the word "golf" but sit outside the traditional game. Removing them keeps a Volkswagen lease or a Topgolf booking from distorting demand for tee times and golf trips.

  • Disc Golf: 6 terms, 4,380 searches.
  • Topgolf Venues: 14 terms, 4,950 searches.
  • Volkswagen Golf: 11 automotive lease and purchase terms, including "vw golf deals" and "golf r pcp," totaling 280 searches at a $3.00 median CPC.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the most expensive golf search terms for advertisers?

International luxury travel queries. "Golf deals fairmont st andrews" leads the dataset at $9.06 per click, followed by Portugal at $8.62 and Scotland at $7.68.

Why are Black Friday golf searches relatively inexpensive per click?

Black Friday searchers focus on discounted retail goods. Those sales leave less margin for each order, so advertisers bid a median $0.95 per click.

Does advertiser CPC vary significantly across search platforms?

Platform variation is small beside the difference in search intent. Median CPC ranges from $0.77 on Instagram to $1.19 on Amazon, with Google at $1.17 and Bing at $1.06.

How can golfers find the best unadvertised green fee rates?

Call course golf shops directly. Off-season passes, twilight rates and weekday afternoon discounts rarely have advertising budgets or dedicated search campaigns.

The Raw Take

Black Friday brings 3,600 monthly searches at $0.95 per click. Fairmont St Andrews brings 40 at $9.06 because those few searchers may be pricing an overseas holiday rather than a discounted box of balls. Golfers hunting a rate that never reaches Google can still try the pro shop and ask for the weekday twilight price.
