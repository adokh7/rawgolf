Jon Rahm PGA Tour return 2027: What is confirmed, what is not

Jon Rahm may leave LIV Golf after 2026, but no verified evidence shows he has agreed PGA Tour terms for 2027; his confirmed May agreement is with the DP World Tour.

Takeaways

  • No PGA Tour primary-source announcement on 18 August 2026 confirms that Jon Rahm has agreed terms for a 2027 return.
  • The PGA TOUR Returning Member Program was strictly limited to the 2026 season, and its application window closed on 2 February 2026.
  • Jon Rahm reached a confirmed agreement with the DP World Tour in May 2026 covering outstanding fines, conditional releases, and agreed appearances.
  • Reports about Rahm leaving LIV Golf remain contested: Telegraph-sourced LIV insiders expect a departure, while Rahm's camp has called the reports speculation.

Has Jon Rahm agreed terms with the PGA Tour?

Jon Rahm has not publicly confirmed a 2027 PGA Tour agreement, and GolfRaw found no PGA Tour primary-source announcement that does so. A report can still prove correct later, but the strongest wording supported on 18 August 2026 is that a PGA Tour agreement is unconfirmed.

A possible LIV exit and a confirmed PGA Tour return are two different claims, and the evidence currently supports only the first as disputed.

On 5 May 2026, the DP World Tour confirmed Rahm accepted conditions covering his outstanding fines, conditional releases for LIV events, and agreed DP World Tour appearances. That arrangement kept open his membership and Ryder Cup eligibility. The only tour agreement verified for Jon Rahm in 2026 is with the DP World Tour, not the PGA Tour.

Can Rahm use the PGA TOUR Returning Member Program for 2027?

The PGA TOUR Returning Member Program was a one-time 2026 reinstatement policy whose application window ran from 12 January to 2 February 2026. The PGA Tour explicitly noted that players could not use the program to indicate intent to return for the 2027 season or beyond.

Rahm declined the opportunity in January, stating on 14 January, "I'm not planning on going anywhere." The window closed on 2 February—197 elapsed calendar days ago. Any 2027 reinstatement requires a completely new policy framework or qualification pathway.

Official Routes That Could Put Rahm on the PGA Tour in 2027

Under current regulations, the top 10 eligible players on the season-ending DP World Tour Race to Dubai eligibility ranking receive PGA Tour cards for the following season. While this provides a theoretical route, the research does not establish that Rahm has secured status via this mechanism.
