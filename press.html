Editorial standards

What the byline and the method mean

Named honestly

Articles, ratings, and tools are credited to GOLFRAW Editorial rather than invented individual bylines. Read the About page.

Ratings are documented

The rating method is public, weighted, and explicit about where judgment sits. Ratings lock at publication. Read the manual.

Mistakes stay visible

Confirmed factual errors are dated and recorded in the public ledger. Check the Corrections Log.

Funding is disclosed

GOLFRAW says it is funded by display advertising served through Google AdSense and does not accept payment for a rating or favourable mention. See the full explanation.
