Charlotte Women's Golf Begins 2026 Season at Quail Hollow

Pinky Chaisilprungruang leads C.C. McMahan's lineup in the 18-hole Truist Summit as the 49ers test their scoring baseline against elite competition on major championship turf.

By GOLFRAW Editorial Published 23 Aug 2026 Updated 23 Aug 2026, 16:15 CEST 6 min read

Key Takeaways

  • Opener Venue: Charlotte launches its 2026–27 season on Monday, 24 August 2026, at Quail Hollow Club in the 18-hole Truist Summit.
  • Lineup Leader: Sophomore Pinky Chaisilprungruang anchors the 49ers after leading the team with a 72.82 scoring average and All-AAC honors last season.
  • Coaching Vision: Head coach C.C. McMahan enters her third season with a veteran core and five returners who logged tournament rounds under par in 2025–26.
  • Championship Test: Quail Hollow’s demanding green complexes and penal fairway bunkering offer an immediate national-caliber benchmark for the team's postseason ambitions.

The Charlotte 49ers women's golf program steps straight into championship-caliber conditions to launch its 2026–27 campaign. On Monday, 24 August 2026, head coach C.C. McMahan's squad takes the first tee at Quail Hollow Club for the 18-hole Truist Summit, squaring off against regional powers on one of North Carolina's premier golf landscapes.

Opening a collegiate season at Quail Hollow offers an unfiltered diagnostic test. The George Cobb design, extensively renovated by Tom Fazio, has tested the world's best across multiple major championships and annual tour stops. For a Charlotte program building towards national postseason relevance in the American Athletic Conference (AAC), starting on firm, fast turf sets an uncompromising standard for ball-striking precision and short-game execution.

Who leads the Charlotte 49ers lineup in 2026?

Sophomore Pinky Chaisilprungruang serves as the focal point of Charlotte's starting five after an impressive debut campaign. The Bangkok native recorded a team-leading 72.82 stroke average, logged 11 rounds of par or better, and captured top-10 finishes at both the Ironwood Invitational and the AAC Championship.

Supporting Chaisilprungruang is a balanced roster of experienced upperclassmen and battle-tested underclassmen. Senior Jette Schulze and fifth-year contributor Mia Gray provide vital tournament maturity, having navigated tough multi-day conference layouts. Coach McMahan has emphasized course management and dispersion control during pre-season practice sessions to eliminate high-variance blow-up holes.

"Starting our year at Quail Hollow demands total commitment on every tee shot. There are no warm-up holes out there, and our players have prepared with the focus required to compete with anyone in the country." C.C. McMahan, Charlotte 49ers Women's Golf Head Coach

How will Quail Hollow test collegiate players?

Quail Hollow Club presents severe penalties for stray approach shots and errant driving angles. With firm bermudagrass fairways bordered by thick rough and lightning-fast Champion Ultradwarf bermudagrass greens, the venue forces players to hit precise landing areas rather than relying solely on length off the tee.

The famed closing stretch—known colloquially as the "Green Mile" across holes 16, 17, and 18—will play a decisive role even in an 18-hole sprint. The long par-4 16th demands a precise fairway wood or hybrid into a guarded green, the par-3 17th forces a forced carry over water, and the par-4 18th requires navigating a winding creek along the entire left side. In collegiate play, managing lag putting on Quail Hollow's undulating surfaces will separate the leaders from the pack.

What is Charlotte's 2026–27 schedule and postseason outlook?

Following Monday's Truist Summit at Quail Hollow, the 49ers travel to several prestigious multi-round events across the Southeast. Key stops include the Cougar Classic at Yeamans Hall Club in Hanahan, South Carolina, and the Ruth's Chris Tar Heel Invitational at UNC Finley Golf Course in Chapel Hill.

Charlotte's primary objective for 2026–27 is securing an at-large bid into the NCAA Division I Women's Golf Regionals. Under the NCAA selection criteria, strength of schedule and head-to-head records against top-50 programs dictate tournament qualification. A commanding performance at Quail Hollow against strong regional competition will give the 49ers immediate momentum in the national collegiate rankings.
