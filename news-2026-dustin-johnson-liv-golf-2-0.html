How close is LIV Golf's investor transaction?

LIV also kept the investor's identity and financial terms private.

A term sheet records the proposed shape of a transaction. Closing requires the parties to finish the binding documents and satisfy the agreed conditions. LIV's public statements do not name those conditions, so no outsider can confirm how much work remains or when the parties might finish it.

How much smaller would the 2027 schedule be?

LIV Golf's proposed 10-event schedule for 2027 would be four events shorter than its 14-event 2026 plan. The arithmetic is 14 minus 10 equals four. Four divided by 14 equals 0.2857, so the proposed schedule would shrink by 28.6%. LIV has not published a final 2027 calendar.

The calculation measures events, not playing days or prize money. LIV moved to 72-hole tournaments in 2026, while the 2027 plan described five international team events and five signature events. A 10-event season could create room for players to compete elsewhere, but LIV has not published the access rules or dates that would make that possible.

Johnson has raised the workload issue before. Reporting after LIV Golf New York said he wanted 18 to 20 starts across a season and would need events beyond a 10-stop LIV calendar. GolfRaw cannot name those extra starts because the public record does not settle his access to other tours.
