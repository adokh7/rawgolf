Wyndham Clark Gary Woodland BMW Championship: Clark Leads by 5

Get the Wyndham Clark Gary Woodland BMW Championship recap. Clark shoots 17-under at Bellerive as Woodland's 199 total protects his Tour Championship bubble spot.

By GOLFRAW Editorial Desk Published 23 Aug 2026 Updated 23 Aug 2026, 15:20 CEST 8 min read

Key takeaways

  • Wyndham Clark carded a 3-under 67 in Round 3 to reach 17-under par (193), securing a five-shot lead entering Sunday at Bellerive Country Club.
  • Gary Woodland shot a third consecutive round in the 60s (67-65-67, 199 total) to sit solo fourth at 11-under par, vaulting from the bubble into projected 23rd in the FedExCup standings.
  • Bellerive's 7,448-yard par-70 layout has yielded lower scoring in 2026 than during the 2018 PGA Championship, driven by softer bentgrass turf and high driving efficiency.
  • Clark's projected victory would place him in the No. 2 starting seed (-8) at the Tour Championship, while Woodland looks to finalize his first trip to East Lake since 2019.

The third round of the 2026 BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club showcased two distinct storylines: Wyndham Clark's relentless charge toward an emphatic five-shot lead, and Gary Woodland's masterclass in postseason survival on the course where he famously held the 36-hole lead at the 2018 PGA Championship.

Clark posted a steady 3-under 67 on Saturday, adding to previous rounds of 66 and 64 to reach 17-under par (193). Playing in the final wave, Clark capitalized on elite ball-striking to keep chasers Rory McIlroy and Patrick Cantlay (-12) at arm's length. Meanwhile, Woodland's 11-under aggregate placed him squarely inside the top 30 cutoff for next week's Tour Championship at East Lake.

How did Wyndham Clark build his 5-shot lead at Bellerive?

Wyndham Clark combined superior driving distance (averaging 326.4 yards off the tee) with precision iron play into Bellerive's receptive bentgrass greens. Entering Saturday with a four-stroke cushion, Clark navigated the difficult four-hole closing stretch without dropping a shot, sinking a 14-foot birdie putt on the par-4 17th to widen his lead to five.

According to PGA Tour ShotLink data, Clark leads the field in Strokes Gained: Off-the-Tee (+4.82) and ranks third in Strokes Gained: Putting (+5.14). His aggressive lines off Bellerive's tree-lined chutes allowed him to attack pins with short irons on holes that forced conservative mid-iron layups from shorter hitters.

Why is Gary Woodland's performance at Bellerive so significant?

Gary Woodland entered the BMW Championship ranked 38th in the FedExCup standings, requiring a minimum two-way tie for fifth place to have any mathematical chance of qualifying for the Tour Championship. Through 54 holes, Woodland sits solo fourth at 11-under par (199), placing him in a projected 23rd position on the live points leaderboard.

Woodland holds a rich competitive history at Bellerive. At the 2018 PGA Championship, Woodland fired an opening 64 and a second-round 66 to hold the 36-hole lead at 10-under par before eventually finishing tied for sixth behind Brooks Koepka. His comfort on Bellerive's expansive corridors and Zoysia fairways was evident throughout Saturday's 3-under 67.

"When you step onto a golf course where you've had success and you see lines that fit your eye, everything feels natural. Bellerive demands commitment off the tee, and I've felt comfortable out here all week." Gary Woodland after his third round at Bellerive Country Club, 22 August 2026

What are the Tour Championship implications for Clark and Woodland?

Sunday's final round at Bellerive carries massive ramifications for the FedExCup Starting Strokes handicap at East Lake. For Wyndham Clark, converting his five-stroke lead into a victory awards 2,000 FedExCup points, propelling him to the No. 2 seed and an 8-under starting baseline in Atlanta—just two shots behind leader Scottie Scheffler.

For Gary Woodland, securing a top-five finish punches his ticket inside the top 30 for the first time since his 2019 U.S. Open victory season. Qualifying for East Lake guarantees exemptions into the 2027 Masters Tournament, 2027 U.S. Open, 2027 Open Championship, and all 2027 PGA Tour Signature Events, securing tens of millions in potential compensation and commercial value.

How do course conditions affect the final round pairings?

Bellerive's turf superintendents will face difficult decisions on Sunday morning regarding green firmness. With temperatures projected in the mid-80s and light winds, aggressive pin placements on tucked corners of the 7th, 11th, and 17th greens will be the primary defense against low scores.

Clark will play in Sunday's final pairing alongside Rory McIlroy, while Patrick Cantlay and Gary Woodland will precede them in the penultimate group. Expect high drama across the front nine as Woodland aims to solidify his Tour Championship spot while McIlroy and Cantlay attempt to apply early scoreboard pressure on Clark.
